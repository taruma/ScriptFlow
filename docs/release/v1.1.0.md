# ScriptFlow v1.1.0 — Detached AI Session History, Cache-Stable Prompts & URL Deep-Linking

> **Tag:** `v1.1.0`  
> **Release Date:** November 26, 2026  
> **Specification:** Auteur Script `v0.4.0` (`AUTEUR_IR_SCHEMA_VERSION = "0.4.0"`, Craft Knowledge Pack `0.4.0-k5`)  
> **GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/taruma/ScriptFlow](https://github.com/taruma/ScriptFlow)

---

## Overview

**ScriptFlow `v1.1.0`** builds on the `v1.0.0` local-first editor and Scene Blueprint foundation with persistent **AI Session History** (including evaluation-ready multi-format exports and expired-interaction recap bridging), a **cache-stable 3-Zone AI prompt architecture** with a unified `Auto` toolset, a **stepped pause-and-resume clarifying interview flow** (`ask_question` + `draft_interview`), a **compact `QuickScanCard` redesign**, and **URL deep-linking** (`?example=`, `?url=`, `?agents=`)—while keeping the **Auteur Script `v0.4.0`** grammar and JSON IR specification locked.

---

## What's New in v1.1.0

### 💾 Detached AI Session History, IndexedDB `v3` & Recap Bridge
- **Independent `aiSessions` Store (`SCRIPT_LIBRARY_DB_VERSION = 3`)**: Auto-saves AI conversations at semantic checkpoints in IndexedDB (with `localStorage` fallback & reconciliation) independently of the `scripts` store, so chat threads survive script deletions and library clears (surfacing a `Script deleted` badge when the source script is gone).
- **Cache-Parity & Trace Deduplication**: Persists text-only `Scene Brief` fields (`specs`, `subtext`, and reference text/notes) while stripping binary media and keeping `omittedAttachmentTag` descriptors for Zone 1 / `previous_interaction_id` cache stability, and deduplicates Zone 1 `systemInstruction` strings in `traceSystemInstructions` for 100% identical `AiTraceModal` inspection.
- **Sidebar Discovery, Sticky Banner & Hover Copy**: Adds a compact **Recent Sessions** card (up to 5 sessions, active script first) in the empty AI sidebar, an icon-only **History** trigger left of `Context` (with a distinct `SquarePen` **New Chat** icon), `File ▾ → AI Chat History...`, a sticky cross-script switch banner pinned above the message stream, and zero-height outside-left user Copy + floating hover assistant Copy buttons.
- **Two-Pane `AiSessionHistoryModal` & `Download | ▾` Exports**: Lazy-loaded modal supporting search, `All` / `This Script` / `Pinned` filters, inline rename/pin/delete, Brief copy, **`Load in Sidebar`**, **`Switch Script & Load`**, and an upward **`Download | ▾`** split menu:
  - **Conversation `.md`** (default 1-click download)
  - **Full Conversation `.json`** (evaluation-ready export with `scriptSnapshot`, `skillsUsed`, `toolsUsed` + compact failure diagnostics, structured suggestion diffs/issues, and per-turn token/cache/cost metrics)
  - **Full Payload `.json`** (raw trace archive with top-level deduplicated `systemInstructions` and `toolsSummary`)
- **Automatic Recap Bridging (`<prior_session_recap>`)**: Detects `'interaction_expired'` errors (`src/services/gemini/errors.ts`) and bridges expired Gemini interaction chains or cross-script continuations deterministically into a fresh chain (`src/services/gemini/sessionRecap.ts`) without mutating frozen Zone 1 `system_instruction`.

### ⚡ Cache-Stable 3-Zone AI Prompt Architecture & Unified `Auto` Toolset (`0.4.0-k4`)
- **Frozen Zone 1 Prefix**: Keeps `tools` + `system_instruction` (Foundational Craft, Pinned Skills, static Tool & Safety Protocol, and On-Demand Skill Directory) byte-for-byte identical across script edits, `Scene Brief` updates, and `Ask First` toggles to maximize Gemini 4 implicit prefix caching.
- **Delta-Only Zone 3 `turnContext`**: Moves volatile per-turn context (`<live_editor_state>` full snapshot vs. compact 1-line `unchanged` confirmation, delta-only `<scene_brief>`, and `<turn_directive>`) into Round 1 `input`, while splitting `ContextPreviewTab` into **System Instruction (Cache-Stable Prefix)** vs. **Next Turn Context (Delta Payload)**.
- **Unified `Auto` Toolset & On-Demand Skills**: Exposes all **10** non-disabled tools consistently in `Auto` focus across empty and non-empty scripts while enforcing `ask_question` at runtime (`Ask First` ON, `[Clarifying follow-up]`, or `Always On`). Removes passive script/brief skill auto-injection so all built-in and custom skills default to **On-Demand** (dynamically loading discontinuous montage exemplars via `load_auteur_skill`), with full backward compatibility across `0.4.0-k1`–`k3` Agent Context Packs.

### 💬 Pause-and-Resume `ask_question`, Stepped `1 / N` Clarifying Flow & `draft_interview` (`0.4.0-k5`)
- **Single-Round Pause & Resume**: `runGeminiAgentTurn` pauses immediately upon `ask_question` success—returning `pendingQuestion` with buffered `siblingFunctionResults` instead of burning an extra Round 2 reply—and resumes the exact `previous_interaction_id` cleanly via `resumeFunctionResults` (with a safe composer/dismiss escape hatch).
- **Stepped `1 / N` `QuestionSuggestionCard`**: Multi-question clarifying flows step through one question at a time with clickable step pills, `Back` / `Next` / `Skip`, `Enter` to advance, `Skip all` / **`Skip & Proceed`**, per-question `multiSelect` (radio vs. checkbox) paired with an independent additive note input, zero resent user chat bubbles, and expandable `✓ Answered` / `Skipped` receipts showing every question together on review.
- **Built-In `draft_interview` Skill**: Added `src/data/knowledge/skills/draft_interview.md` (default On-Demand, `AUTEUR_KNOWLEDGE_VERSION = "0.4.0-k5"`) guiding 2–4 high-leverage directorial questions before drafting open scenes.

### 🔍 Compact `QuickScanCard` Redesign & 4-Theme Parity
- **Independent Whole-Card Collapse**: Folds the Quick Scan card into a calm 1-line summary bar without dismissing Live Mode `AiQuickScanIndicator` margin dots.
- **Canonical Lens Order & Issues-First Drill-Down**: Renders `Lens 1`–`Lens 5` + `Scene Brief & Media` in canonical order, collapsed by default with **`Expand all` / `Collapse all`**, issues-first expansion with `Show N passed checks` reveal, probability micro-bars, 4-theme `auteur-phase-*` status tokens, and aligned footer actions (**`Deep Audit Selected`** + quiet `Re-scan` / `Dismiss`).

### 🔗 URL Deep-Linking (`/?example=`, `/?url=`, `/?agents=`) & Readable Example Slugs
- **Readable Bundled Example Slugs**: Added unique `slug` identifiers in `src/data/examples/catalog.json` (`the-grand-continental`, `mark-and-lucas`, `balloon-panic`, `robert-machu-pichu`, `three-years-late-part-1`..`4`, `frequency-over-force-part-1`..`2`) and `getBundledExampleByIdOrSlug()`.
- **Mutually Exclusive Launch Routing (`src/ui/urlLaunch.ts`)**: Validates single-action URL parameters (`http:`/`https:` checks, `history.replaceState` query cleanup, and CORS/size-bounded remote text fetching):
  - **Desktop (`>= 640px`)**: `/?example=` opens the bundled reference in read-only mode; `/?url=` fetches and opens `ScriptImportModal` pre-loaded with its pre-flight scorecard; `/?agents=` fetches and opens `AgentContextImportDialog` without auto-opening the AI sidebar.
  - **Narrow Viewports (`< 640px`)**: Modals stay disabled; `/?example=` and `/?url=` (opening as a read-only `'remote'` preview without touching IndexedDB or `localStorage`) are supported, while `/?agents=` is ignored with a toast.
